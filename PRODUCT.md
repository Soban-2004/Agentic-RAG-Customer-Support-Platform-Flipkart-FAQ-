# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Product Purpose
A customer support assistant that answers Flipkart policy questions and supports order, refund, and human escalation workflows. Existing React and FastAPI functionality remains the implementation authority.

## Users
Repository-supported audiences: customers seeking support and evaluators trying the public demo. Their relative priority is undecided.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Authenticated conversation history, streamed responses, markdown answers, anonymous demo, and light/dark/system theme settings. Demo conversations are unsaved. Backend order operations use demonstration data; do not invent live service claims.

## Brand Commitments
The user approved an independent visual identity and a rich, cinematic first impression on 2026-10-14. The product must stand out through color, typography, effects, and animation. Flipkart remains the subject of supported policies and example orders, rather than the UI brand.

## Product Principles
- Make the visual identity distinctive and consistent across entry and chat screens.
- Preserve existing support capabilities and routes.
- Make demonstration content and connection states truthful.

## Evidence on Hand
README.md, frontend/src, and the existing FAQ datasets establish supported product functionality. No customer testimonials or commercial performance claims are supplied.
